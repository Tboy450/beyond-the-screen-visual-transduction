## Voice and Editorial Review

The follow-up revision revisited the earlier editorial framing in response
to the author's direction to preserve a skeptical, exploratory voice.

- Reworked passages that treated an unresolved mechanism as a reason to narrow the discussion.
- Removed unsupported rankings such as “the most realistic path” and dismissive comparisons with richer perception concepts.
- Applied scrutiny to official descriptions and claims of impossibility as well as claims of capability.
- Kept reported experience, perceptual richness, and learning in view alongside practical task performance.
- Expanded the [evidence guide](sources/evidence-guide.md) around specific mechanisms, competing explanations, and open questions.

## Technical Clarifications

The revisions distinguish event-camera brightness changes from interpreted
scene events, clarify the simplified retinal circuit, add scoped summaries
of selected studies, and identify proposed designs without implying a
documented implementation. Existing scientific and historical material
remains available for further author review.
