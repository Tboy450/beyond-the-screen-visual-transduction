# DARPA Research: Nimbus, NIMBUS, and the Missing Middle

[Book home](README.md) · [Contents](CONTENTS.md) · [Research map](RESEARCH_MAP.md)

What happens between a signal and an experience? Much of this encyclopedia
lives in that middle territory: conversion, addressing, biological response,
and the part perception might supply. DARPA's research programs give us
concrete attempts to change pieces of that chain. They also give us claims
to examine: what was requested, what was built, what was measured, and which
conclusions remain arguments rather than observations.

## Which Nimbus?

DARPA has used the name for two distinct efforts. Keeping the full titles
visible makes the research trail easier to follow.

| Name | Documented focus | Connection to this book |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Nimbus](https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/nimbus) | Lightning, associated emissions including X-rays, and upper-atmospheric phenomena. | Discharges, energy conversion, and the relationship between an event and its measurable signatures. |
| [NIMBUS: NIMBle Ultrafast microSystems](https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/nimble-ultrafast-microsystems) | Micromechanical oscillators for positioning and navigation. | Resonance, transducer geometry, and the physical limits of a particular device architecture. |
| [N³: Next-Generation Nonsurgical Neurotechnology](https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/next-generation-nonsurgical-neurotechnology) | Bidirectional brain–machine interfaces without surgical implantation. | Reading neural activity and delivering input through optical, acoustic, or electromagnetic routes. |
| [NESD: Neural Engineering System Design](https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/neural-engineering-system-design) | High-resolution neural interfaces, with visual and auditory restoration among the aims. | Encoding sensory information, converting it locally, and connecting electronics with neural processing. |

These descriptions were checked on **6 November 2026**. DARPA marks the
lightning Nimbus, N³, and NESD pages as completed programs. Its
[HALOVS overview](https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/halovs) places
NIMBUS in a vibratory-sensor portfolio and reports that the portfolio's
solicitations are closed. A program's administrative status and the results
of its experiments are separate records.

## Nimbus: Read the Event Through Its Emissions

The lightning program asked how discharges initiate, propagate, and attach
to objects, and how they relate to upper-atmospheric effects. Its remit
included understanding emissions that accompany the event.
[DARPA's archived description](https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/nimbus)
provides that research context.

For this encyclopedia, the useful question is broader: **which part of a
physical process are we actually observing?** A detector can reveal one
signature while leaving another part of the mechanism unresolved. That
distinction belongs beside the [carrier](modules/03-carriers.md) and
[transducer](modules/04-transducers.md) chapters. It also matters when a
measurement of neural activity is used to interpret an experience.

The connection is methodological. To carry it into a proposed interface,
identify the particular emission, receiver, conversion, and information
content that the analogy suggests.

## NIMBUS: Is the Limit in the Material or the Arrangement?

The microsystems effort asks how far small mechanical resonators can be
pushed. The [December 2024 FAQ](https://www.darpa.mil/sites/default/files/attachment/2024-12/nimbus-faq-12-19-24.pdf)
clarifies that its scope extends beyond MEMS gyroscopes, that the velocity
targets concern peak motion, and that alternative materials need their own
justified limits. It also acknowledges that the solicitation did not provide
specific references for some stated benchmark values. That is a useful place
to begin a skeptical reading: trace a supposed limit to its calculation,
material, geometry, and measurement.

There is now published work to examine. **Kaya and colleagues (2026)** report
a piezoelectric microacoustic structure with motion localized at a
topological interface. They measured a particle velocity of **51.3 m/s** and
explicitly acknowledge NIMBUS funding. Here, particle velocity describes
local oscillatory motion in the structure. The authors attribute the gain
to the vibration mode's compliance and energy localization.
[Primary paper, published 11 July 2026](https://doi.org/10.1002/adfm.76905).

That result suggests an important design question: when an interface seems
to have reached a limit, have we exhausted the material, or only the way we
are driving and arranging it?

For a perception concept, the next question might concern a mechanical
scanner, a tactile element, or a conversion stage that concentrates motion
where it is useful. Those are extensions proposed here. Their value would
depend on displacement, frequency, controllability, losses, and the needs of
the receiver. A large velocity number is one part of that comparison.

## N³: Several Ways to Cross the Interface

N³ is the closer match when the question is neural communication. Its
program description set a target of reading and writing 16 independent
channels in a small tissue volume within 50 milliseconds. These were stated
system objectives; evaluating the outcome requires the corresponding
experiment and its measurements.
[DARPA program description](https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/next-generation-nonsurgical-neurotechnology).

The [2019 team announcement](https://www.darpa.mil/news/2019/nonsurgical-brain-machine-interfaces)
distinguished entirely external approaches from “minutely invasive” ones
using delivered transducers. Three of the announced routes illustrate the
range of conversion problems:

| Team | Announced approach | Question for the encyclopedia |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Battelle | An external transceiver paired with electromagnetic nanotransducers for bidirectional conversion. | Can conversion close to the target change what the external carrier must accomplish? |
| PARC | Ultrasound combined with magnetic fields to produce local electrical effects. | Could two interacting fields provide selectivity that either field alone lacks? |
| Teledyne | Optical-pumping magnetometers for recording, with focused ultrasound for stimulation. | How would separately measured read and write channels become a coordinated loop? |

The announcement described wearable systems communicating over millimeter
distances. That geometry is part of the proposed mechanism and should travel
with the description. The table records the teams' announced approaches;
the questions in its final column are this book's extensions.

For perception, an especially interesting possibility is that recording and
stimulation could be used together to adjust a code as the user learns it.
Would that loop seek to reproduce a familiar sensory pattern, discover a
new learnable pattern, or move between the two? What would tell us whether
the device, the person, or both had adapted?

## NESD: Vision Beyond an Electrode Grid

NESD makes the sensory question explicit. Its program page described goals
of recording from a million neurons, stimulating a hundred thousand, and
interacting bidirectionally with a thousand. These numbers concern an
interface ambition; the relationship between neural channels and experience
is itself a question to investigate.
[DARPA program description](https://www.darpa.mil/research/programs/neural-engineering-system-design).

DARPA's [July 2017 announcement](https://www.darpa.mil/news/2017/mplantable-neural-interface)
identified four teams focused on vision. Their proposed routes went beyond
a single model of electrode stimulation:

| Team | Proposed visual-interface route |
| --- | --- |
| Columbia | A flexible electronic array at the cortical surface, with wireless power and communication. |
| Fondation Voir et Entendre | Camera input linked to optogenetic cortical stimulation through implanted electronics and micro-LEDs. |
| John B. Pierce Laboratory | Modified neurons using bioluminescent reporting and optogenetic response in an optical interface. |
| University of California, Berkeley | Holographic optical recording and stimulation informed by models of responses to sensory input. |

These approaches give the book's “biological phosphor,” local conversion,
and perceptual-encoding questions concrete research counterparts. The
architecture matters even before its full performance is settled: it names
where a conversion might happen and what a competing implementation would
need to do differently.

## What Would These Programs Let Us Ask Next?

The following are concept-development questions, offered for comparison
with the [hypothesis registry](HYPOTHESIS_REGISTRY.md).

**Could the receiver provide part of the addressing?** An external field
need not carry every spatial distinction if a local converter or responsive
cell population supplies some selectivity. The useful comparison would
separate selectivity in the carrier, converter, and biological response.

**Could timing organize a richer percept than a static channel count
suggests?** A count of independently controlled sites may miss what ordered
sequences, adaptation, or learned transitions could contribute. Compare
spatially richer input with temporally richer input while accounting for
the information delivered and the training provided.

**Could perception supply a different kind of resolution?** If a person
learns a stable relationship between sparse cues and the world, what changes:
the number of distinguishable sensations, recognition of structure, fluency
of action, or the reported quality of experience? These deserve separate
observations. Practical task performance need not be the only question.

**Which failures belong to the apparatus?** A poor result could arise from
weak coupling, unstable positioning, an unsuitable code, the measurement,
or an incorrect account of perception. Specify which explanation a test
actually distinguishes before treating it as a limit on the whole idea.

## Following the Record

Official documents establish what an institution publicly proposed and
reported. Papers expose methods and measurements that can be examined more
closely. Both remain open to criticism. A funding announcement, a benchmark,
and a completed-program label answer different questions.

For a disputed claim, keep the proposed mechanism visible, identify the
strongest supporting result and the strongest competing explanation, and
state what observation would change the assessment. An unanswered question
can stay unanswered. Claims of capability and claims of impossibility each
need their own support.
