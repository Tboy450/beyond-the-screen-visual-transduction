# Annotated Research Notes

Short notes on why each source matters to the encyclopedia.

## CRT And Display Physics

### National MagLab: Cathode Ray Electromagnetic Deflection Basics

Useful for the CRT baseline. It supports the idea that a cathode-ray tube is a
controlled beam system where electric or magnetic fields can steer charged
particles. This anchors the CRT-as-transduction-machine analogy.

Source:
https://nationalmaglab.org/magnet-academy/watch-play/interactive-tutorials/cathode-ray-electromagnetic-deflection-basics/

### Samsung Display: Polarizer

Useful for LCD background. It supports the explanation that LCDs depend on
polarization control rather than direct light emission.

Source:
https://global.samsungdisplay.com/28774/

### U.S. Department Of Energy: OLED Basics

Useful for OLED background. It supports the distinction between backlit displays
and emissive pixels.

Source:
https://www.energy.gov/cmei/ssl/oled-basics

## Visual System Foundations

### NCBI Bookshelf: Neuroanatomy, Visual Pathway

Useful for the visual pathway module. It anchors the route from retina to optic
nerve, optic chiasm, optic tract, LGN, optic radiations, and visual cortex.

Source:
https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK553189/

### Webvision: Phototransduction In Rods And Cones

Useful for the retina module. It supports the idea that the retina is an active
biological transducer, not merely a passive projection surface.

Source:
https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK52768/

### Webvision: The Primary Visual Cortex

Useful for V1 and retinotopy. It helps ground the distinction between a cortical
map and a literal display grid.

Source:
https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK11524/

## Retinal Prostheses

### Weitz et al.: Improving The Spatial Resolution Of Epiretinal Implants

Useful for explaining why retinal prostheses are not simple pixel displays.
It supports the theme that spatial resolution, electrode interactions, and
retinal coding are central bottlenecks.

Source:
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4698804/

### Webvision: Retinal Prostheses

Useful as a broad educational reference for retinal prosthesis types,
challenges, and clinical context.

Source:
https://www.webvision.pitt.edu/book/part-xv-prosthetics/retinal-prostheses-by-ethan-d-cohen/

## Cortical Prostheses

### Bosking et al.: Electrical Stimulation Of Visual Cortex

Useful for cortical visual prosthesis background. It supports discussion of
phosphenes, cortical stimulation, and the relevance of visual cortex mapping.

Source:
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6916716/

### Beauchamp et al.: Dynamic Stimulation Of Visual Cortex Produces Form Vision

Useful for the cortical form-tracing scenario. It anchors the idea that dynamic
stimulation may produce more coherent form percepts than isolated static
phosphenes.

Source:
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7331799/

## Optogenetics

### Sahel et al.: Partial Recovery Of Visual Function After Optogenetic Therapy

Useful for optogenetic retina discussion. It supports optogenetics as a real
research path for partial visual function restoration.

Source:
https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-021-01351-4

### Provansal et al.: Vision Restoration By Optogenetic Therapy

Useful as background on optogenetic strategies, gene delivery, and restoration
concepts.

Source:
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8762673/

## Magnetic, Ultrasound, And Wireless Concepts

### Lee et al.: Transcranial Focused Ultrasound Stimulation Of Human V1

Useful for focused-ultrasound visual-interface discussion. It gives grounding
for ultrasound as a research carrier for visual cortex modulation.

Source:
https://www.nature.com/articles/srep34026

### Tapia et al.: Phosphene-Guided TMS

Useful for magnetic phosphene discussion. It connects TMS and visual cortex
phosphene targeting.

Source:
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4039568/

### Kim et al.: Magnetoelectric Nanodiscs

Useful for local-transducer speculation. It supports the general concept that
wireless fields can be paired with local material transducers in neuromodulation
research.

Source:
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11750723/

## Sensory Substitution And Event-Based Encoding

### Bach-y-Rita et al.: Vision Substitution By Tactile Image Projection

Useful as a classic sensory substitution anchor. It supports the idea that
visual information can be translated into another sensory channel and learned.

Source:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/5818337/

### Nau et al.: BrainPort Artificial Vision Device

Useful for tactile substitution and training questions. It supports the idea
that learned tactile visual substitution can produce useful perception-like
behavior.

Source:
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4281706/

### Tayarani-Najaran And Schmuker: Event-Based Sensing

Useful for event-stream concepts. It supports the idea of representing sensory
information as changes over time rather than full frames.

Source:
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8203204/
